**Local LLMs** are no longer science projects. They are production-ready tools running on the laptop in your backpack.

## What Are Local LLMs and On-Device AI?

**Local LLMs** are large language models optimized to run on consumer hardware—no API keys, no cloud bills, no data leaving your machine. **On-Device AI** takes this further, embedding models directly into phones, browsers, and edge devices.

Three releases catalyzed this shift:
- **Llama 2**: Meta’s open weights sparked the open-source explosion.
- **Mistral 7B**: Proved small models can outperform giants.
- **Gemini Nano**: Google put a multimodal model on Pixel phones.

## Why On-Device AI Matters for Your Career

If you are transitioning into AI, this is your entry point. Cloud ML engineering requires Kubernetes, GPUs, and distributed systems. Local inference requires Python, `llama.cpp`, and curiosity.

**Four reasons to care:**
1. **Privacy by default**: Medical, legal, and financial data never leaves the device.
2. **Zero marginal cost**: Run inference 10,000 times. The bill is $0.
3. **Offline-first apps**: Build tools that work in subways, airplanes, and secure facilities.
4. **Differentiation**: "Shipped a local RAG pipeline" beats "Called OpenAI API" on a resume.

## Getting Started: Tools and Frameworks

Don’t train. Quantize. Quantization compresses 16-bit weights to 4-bit integers with minimal quality loss.

**Your starter stack:**
- **llama.cpp / Ollama**: Run GGUF models in one command. `ollama run mistral`
- **LM Studio**: GUI for model management, chat, and local OpenAI-compatible server.
- **Transformers + bitsandbytes**: For Python-native workflows and fine-tuning.
- **MediaPipe / TensorFlow Lite**: Deploy Gemini Nano or custom TFLite models to Android.
- **WebLLM / Transformers.js**: Run LLMs entirely in the browser via WebGPU.

**Weekend project**: Build a local RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) app. Ingest your PDFs. Query them offline. Ship a Tauri/Electron desktop app.

## Challenges and Trade-offs

Local AI isn't magic. Know the limits:
- **Context windows**: Most local models cap at 4k–32k tokens. Cloud offers 128k–1M+.
- **Multimodality**: Rare locally. Gemini Nano is the exception, not the rule.
- **Hardware fragmentation**: Apple Silicon (Metal) works seamlessly. NVIDIA (CUDA) works fast. AMD/Intel/Windows? Often painful.
- **Maintenance burden**: You manage model updates, quantization choices, and prompt templates.
