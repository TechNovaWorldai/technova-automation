🐦 Twitter via Buffer — Setup Guide
Free Plan

Twitter's own API costs $100/month — Buffer's free plan is the practical alternative.

  1. Sign up free at buffer.com and connect your Twitter/X account (free plan: 3 channels, 10 queued posts each).
  2. Click "🐦 Export Twitter → Buffer CSV" above — downloads all pending Twitter posts from this queue.
  3. In Buffer: open your Twitter channel → Settings (⚙️) → General → Bulk Upload → download Buffer's CSV template once (column order must match).
  4. Upload the file you exported from here. Buffer shows a preview — review for errors, then set times for each post.
  5. Click "Add Posts to Queue" — Buffer auto-posts at the scheduled times.

⚠️ Buffer's bulk upload caps at 100 posts per import and free plan allows 10 queued per channel — well within a weekly batch of 25 tweets.
